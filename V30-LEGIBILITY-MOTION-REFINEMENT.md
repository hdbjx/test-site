# V30 Homepage Refinement

This pass keeps the V29 direction while correcting the legibility and pacing problems visible in the rendered preview.

## Key changes
- Exact Every Detail brand red sampled from the logo asset: `#D56865`.
- Hero keeps the centered poster composition but adds a soft animated focus field behind the headline, calmer image luminance, tighter line sizing, and subtle pointer-driven line rotation.
- Removed the flat post-hero bridge and replaced it with a compact curved SVG ribbon.
- Ribbon moves continuously on its own and receives a large additional offset from page scroll.
- Service chooser moved closer to the fold so the first actual option appears faster.
- Work/archive heading fades and exits before the final archive statement enters.
- Rebuilt the student story as two readable sentence lines with photo slots inside the typography. Images still arrive from different directions but cannot cover the core words.
- Reduced review scale and added lighter scroll/pointer depth.
- Reduced specialty headline sizes and top-level marquee dominance to prevent clipping.
- Preserved the successful final driveway/detail-shop scene.

## Motion rule
Motion can overlap in time, but important text must never overlap other important text enough to compromise comprehension.
