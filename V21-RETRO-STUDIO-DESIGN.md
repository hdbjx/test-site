# Every Detail V21 — Retro Studio Design

V21 keeps V20's cleaner layout, accessibility, responsive behavior, and conversion-oriented UI, then restores Every Detail's retro automotive identity as a controlled brand layer.

## Direction
- 1970s automotive editorial + modern web studio execution
- Cream, ink, and Every Detail red
- Print rules, compact uppercase labels, subtle paper grain
- Photography framed like contact sheets, prints, and magazine features
- Selective offset shadows rather than shadows on every component
- Square/near-square editorial geometry for brand surfaces
- Modern, predictable interaction design for booking and forms

## Page language
- Services: vintage garage service menu
- About: automotive magazine profile
- Our Work: photo lab/contact-sheet cues
- Service Areas: route guide / field guide
- Paint Correction + Ceramic: workshop spec sheet
- Detail+: members club
- Gift Cards: physical certificate
- Blog: automotive publication
- Booking: modern product UI with small booking-desk cues
- Legal: restrained editorial print

## Guardrails
Retro is not an excuse for clutter. No random rotations, no thick outline around every object, no distressed texture over body copy, no excessive shadows, and no decorative treatment that makes a control harder to understand or use.
