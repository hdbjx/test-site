# V102 — Friendly Retro Premium Opening Card

## What changed

- Removed the V101 timed split-screen transformation completely.
- The first-visit card now arrives as one finished warm-paper composition.
- Replaced the corporate red poster panel with smaller retro brand details: a tilted red ticket, Every Detail round mark, warm paper texture, editorial type, and restrained physical depth.
- Vehicle search is visible immediately. Customers no longer wait for the layout to transform before they can act.
- Reduced the opening interaction to one soft card entrance and a very small content stagger.
- Updated the welcome copy to feel friendlier and more personal: "Your car makes this site yours."
- Simplified account actions into rounded secondary controls instead of boxed dashboard buttons.
- Updated the search autofocus delay from 2.3 seconds to 520ms to match the shorter entrance.
- Preserved the V101 day-of email account/live-status module unchanged.
- Change-vehicle flow remains unchanged.

## Motion rule

No animated layout dimensions, grid-track interpolation, headline scaling, or timed panel reveals. The card has a short entrance only. Reduced-motion users receive the final composition immediately.
