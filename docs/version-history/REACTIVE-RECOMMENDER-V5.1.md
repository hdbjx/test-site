# Reactive Recommender V5.1

- Recommendation and price stay hidden until the customer chooses timing.
- Timing is a single, low-friction commitment step.
- Recommendation copy reacts to vehicle, condition, concerns, and timing.
- Contact capture appears only after timing and recommendation reveal.
- Contact CTA reacts to ASAP, weekend, next week, or exploring intent.
- First-name entry immediately personalizes the capture card.
- Quote capture requires name, phone, and email. Address remains a booking-only requirement.
- Existing behavioral CRM events are preserved: assessment_completed, timing_selected, recommendation_viewed, contact_captured, availability_viewed.
- Existing Team App behavioral CRM remains compatible with these events.
