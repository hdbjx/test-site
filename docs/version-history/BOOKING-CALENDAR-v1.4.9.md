# Booking Calendar v1.4.9

Simplifies the booking calendar while keeping the date unmistakable.

- Shows one month at a time instead of two calendars side by side.
- Adds simple previous/next month controls only when the booking window crosses months.
- Removes the large instruction header, legend, availability dots, and boxed date styling.
- Uses a familiar calendar layout: unavailable dates are muted, bookable dates are clear, and the selected date is solid Every Detail red.
- Keeps the selected full date and available arrival times inside the same compact card.
- Leaves Supabase availability and booking logic unchanged.
