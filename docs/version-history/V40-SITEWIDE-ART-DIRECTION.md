# V40 — Sitewide Art Direction

This pass extends the homepage visual system across the entire Every Detail website without forcing every route into the same layout.

## Shared system
- V39 red / cream / ink palette
- compact pull-down navigation on desktop and full-screen menu on mobile
- unified page-header composition
- unified closing CTA and footer
- subtle paper grain and controlled image depth
- section reveal timing and pointer response on expressive pages
- reduced-motion support
- square controls and 16px mobile form inputs on transactional pages

## Expressive pages
About, Our Work, Detail+, Paint Correction, Ceramic, and Gift Cards retain their own content but now use larger editorial typography, deliberate color takeovers, stronger photography, and V39-style section contrast.

## Decision/content pages
Services, Mobile Detailing, Service Areas, area detail pages, Blog, and posts use dark editorial openings with cream/red typography, quieter content sections, stronger hover states, and clearer conversion hierarchy.

## Transactional pages
Booking, quote, account, sign-in, password update, Detail+ join, and client-info forms keep motion restrained. They use the same brand palette, typography, header/footer system, form styling, and spacing without introducing unnecessary scroll effects.

## Responsive
The shared system has explicit breakpoints for desktop, tablet, and phone. Large headlines, menus, CTA bands, footer layouts, and page headers all recompose rather than merely shrink.
