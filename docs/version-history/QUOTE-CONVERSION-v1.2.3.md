# Quote conversion funnel v1.2.3

- Changes quiz CTA to "See my price".
- Broadens condition question from interior-only to whole-car condition.
- Simplifies the recommendation result around service, inclusions and price.
- Makes Name, Phone and Email required before the booking CTA is revealed.
- "Save my quote" is the primary lead-capture conversion and submits directly to the existing website quote/CRM pipeline.
- After capture, "Book this detail" becomes the dominant CTA.
- Add-ons and paint services move behind an optional post-save customization panel.
- Customization changes can be saved back to the same CRM lead.
- Generic quote submissions now also require email server-side.
