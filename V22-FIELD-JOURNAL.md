# Every Detail V22 — Field Journal

V22 moves the site away from recognizable SaaS / AI landing-page composition and toward a bespoke automotive publication built around Every Detail itself.

## Direction

- Retro is treated as editorial art direction, not a texture pack.
- The homepage behaves like a publication: cover, service manual, field report, company note, workshop ledger, print advert, classifieds, back matter, back cover.
- Real photography is allowed to dominate instead of being trapped in repeated rounded cards.
- Cream, black, and Every Detail red remain the core palette.
- Typography uses extreme scale contrast and editorial serif moments rather than gradient type or trendy glass treatments.
- Buttons and editorial surfaces are squared off. Product surfaces such as booking remain calmer and usability-first.
- No glassmorphism, bento-for-bento's-sake, gradient blobs, floating stat cards, glowing CTAs, pill soup, or generic feature icon grids were added.

## Homepage changes

- Hero reframed as the cover of the Every Detail Field Journal.
- Proof strip becomes a compact red masthead ticker.
- Service selection sits inside a service-manual frame rather than a generic marketing section.
- Work photography becomes a dark field report with magazine furniture and captions.
- Team section becomes a company field note.
- How It Works becomes a workshop ledger with large sequence numbers instead of three matching cards.
- Gift cards become an inserted print advert.
- Paint care and Detail+ read like editorial/classified notices rather than SaaS product cards.
- FAQ is styled as publication back matter.
- Final CTA is intentionally sparse and oversized, like a back cover.

## Secondary pages

Existing V21 page-specific identities remain, with V22 adding publication folios and more editorial body typography. Services retains the service-manual direction; Our Work retains contact-sheet language; About remains a magazine profile; paint/ceramic retain technical workshop cues; Detail+ remains a members-club artifact; Gift Cards retain certificate cues.

## Product UX guardrail

Booking, quote, account, and form behavior are not made deliberately retro. Brand cues may surround these flows, but interaction patterns remain conventional, accessible, and conversion-focused.
