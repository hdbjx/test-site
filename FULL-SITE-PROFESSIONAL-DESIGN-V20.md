# Every Detail V20 — Full-Site Professional Design Pass

This pass extends the V19 studio direction beyond the homepage and across the customer-facing website.

## Art direction
- Unified warm paper, charcoal, muted neutral and restrained Every Detail red palette.
- Replaced heavy poster-style borders, offset shadows and rotations with cleaner editorial surfaces.
- Standardized rounded controls and cards, lighter 1 px rules, quieter shadows and more whitespace.
- Preserved the existing page architecture and booking/business logic.

## Page families addressed
- Services and Mobile Car Detailing
- About Us
- Our Work
- Service Areas and location pages
- Paint Correction and Ceramic
- Detail+
- Gift Cards
- Blog
- Privacy and Terms
- Booking, quote, information and account/form surfaces
- Shared navigation, buttons, forms and footer

## UX intent
Marketing pages now read as a single premium automotive brand system. Transactional pages intentionally use a calmer product-interface language so booking and lead capture stay clear and fast.
