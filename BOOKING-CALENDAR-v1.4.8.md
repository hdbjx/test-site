# Booking Calendar v1.4.8

Replaces the horizontal date-card strip with a recognizable month calendar.

- Full Sunday-Saturday calendar grid with month/year headings.
- Available dates are visibly highlighted and unavailable dates are muted.
- Selected date uses the Every Detail red treatment and stays visually obvious.
- Selected date is repeated in full above the arrival-time choices, e.g. "Friday, October 16".
- Availability count and Eastern-time note remain visible.
- Next-31-day availability logic and booking backend are unchanged.
- Responsive: two calendar months side-by-side on larger screens and stacked on mobile.
