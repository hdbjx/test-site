# V65 Vehicle Catalog Expansion

Expanded the sitewide vehicle catalog from 963 parsed entries to 1,434 entries while preserving the V64 sitewide vehicle-context system.

Focus areas:
- mainstream legacy and current models
- newer EVs and electrified variants
- performance models and common named trims
- midsize and heavy-duty trucks
- passenger and work vans
- broader crossover/SUV coverage

All additions use the existing Every Detail pricing classes: sedan, smallSUV, largeSUV, smallTruck, largeTruck, and minivan. The V64 manual pricing-class override remains available for edge cases.

No visual or funnel behavior changes in this version.
