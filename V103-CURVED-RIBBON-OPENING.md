# V103 — Curved Ribbon Opening

The first-visit vehicle card now uses the homepage's curved marquee language as one-time entrance choreography instead of transforming the card itself.

## Motion
- Upper coral ribbon enters from the right and settles behind the card's upper edge.
- Lower coral ribbon enters from the left and settles across the foreground near the card's lower edge.
- Ribbons do not loop after arrival.
- The card only fades/rises a few pixels and never resizes or morphs.
- Reduced-motion users see the final composition immediately.

## Card refinement
- Removed the floating red ticket because the ribbons now provide the expressive red element.
- Tightened the card height and overall footprint.
- Re-composed the headline into three intentional lines: "Your car makes / this site / yours."
- Shortened supporting copy and kept it above the fold.
- Replaced "One quick thing" with "01 / Your car".
- Softened the search-field focus shadow from the heavier V102 treatment.
- Preserved account sign-in/create-account and remember-car behavior.

## Preserved
- V100 reactive header system.
- V99 site-wide depth system.
